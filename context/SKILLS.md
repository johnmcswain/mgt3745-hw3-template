Two logs in one file. First, what *you* can do and attest to: the capabilities you have
demonstrated, so that you know which delegated outputs you can actually inspect. Second,
the dependencies you evaluated and why you chose or rejected each one.

Module 5 adds a dependency-evaluation lab using npm packages with real drama in their
histories. Some of them will be in your project by December. Choose carefully.

# QUESTIONS.md

**Optional. Not graded on HW3.**

Your HW2 handoff test asked what a stranger would still have to ask you. This file keeps that
list alive. Every open question about the project, dated, with who could answer it. Closed
questions stay in the file with their answers. Over a semester this becomes the most useful
document in the repo, and the one almost nobody keeps.

## Open
- [ ] [Question] (asked YYYY-MM-DD; who can answer)

## Closed
- [x] [Question] (asked YYYY-MM-DD; answered YYYY-MM-DD: [answer])
